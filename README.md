# 🧺 MySusu – Digital Savings Platform for Everyday Liberians

**MySusu** (also known as *SaveMama*) is a digital savings platform designed to help everyday people in Liberia — especially market women, students, and low-income earners — save money securely and conveniently through a mobile-friendly web application.

---

## 🚀 Features

- ✅ **Short-Term & Long-Term Savings**
  - Save anytime or lock savings for 1–3 months.
  
- 🎯 **Goal-Based Saving**
  - Create savings goals like:
    - School fees
    - Rent
    - Business capital
    - Travel
    - Medical bills

- 💳 **Virtual SaveMama Card**
  - See your real-time savings balance and account details.

- 📜 **Transaction History**
  - View all deposits and withdrawals with dates and purposes.

- 📲 **Mobile Money Integration** *(Coming Soon)*
  - Deposit/Withdraw using MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money.

- 🔔 **Smart Notifications** *(Coming Soon)*
  - Weekly or monthly reminders via SMS or WhatsApp.

- 🛠️ **Admin Dashboard**
  - Manage users, monitor total savings, and send messages.

---

## 🌍 Why It Matters

Many people in Liberia still save money under their mattresses or with informal "susu" collectors. **MySusu** offers a secure, transparent, mobile-first alternative — encouraging savings, building trust, and promoting financial literacy.
